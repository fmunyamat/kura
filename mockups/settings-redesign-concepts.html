Settings, ten ways

Ten structural takes on the Settings tab, all in the Clear Sky light theme and built from tokens that already exist in theme.ts. Every concept keeps the same real content — grass type, effort tier, log out, and the red "I've moved" reset — so they're directly comparable. Concepts 3 and 6 sketch one optional read-only extra (season) to show how the layout grows; nothing else is invented.

01
Lawn Passport

The whole profile is one identity card — crest, name, and attribute lines with a dashed perforation, like a plant passport. Strongest "my lawn is a thing I own" feel; the account rows stay quiet below it.
