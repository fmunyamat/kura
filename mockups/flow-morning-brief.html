How the loop works

▶ Tap the task card to expand its full details
▶ Click “Mark as done ✓” to advance
1
Land on today
One hero task, condensed: photo, name, time. Tapping the card slides open the full why + how-to steps; tapping again tucks them away.
2
Mark as done
A lime sweep grows out of the button and a checkmark draws itself — the reward lands exactly where the finger already is.
3
Next task promotes
The finished card slides off left; the “up next” task springs up into the hero slot with its own photo. The queue row refills with tomorrow.
4
Day complete
When the queue empties, a ring seals the day, confetti falls, and the streak chip ticks 6 → 7 with a pop. Tomorrow is previewed so there's always a next step.
