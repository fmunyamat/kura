Kura · Today Screen — Light Glassmorphism

Three frosted takes, one glass system.

Fresh Cut, Sun & Shade and Clear Sky, now sharing a single glassmorphism recipe: every surface is a translucent frosted pane with a real backdrop blur and saturation boost, a bright light-catching top edge fading to a faint base, an inset sheen line, and a soft drop shadow. Only the wash colour, accent and fill tint change between them — the glass behaves the same everywhere.

01Fresh Cut

High-key milk-glass. The whitest frost of the three so panes read bright and clean, with a vivid grass-green accent. Airy — but now a true translucent pane with the lawn saturating through.
