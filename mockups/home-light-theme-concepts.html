Kura · Today Screen — Light Theme Board

Six ways to see it in daylight.

The build today only has the dusk version — a blurred photo under a dark green wash. These six repaint the same screen (greeting, streak/weather card, progress bar, task list, floating nav) for a light theme, each with its own logic for what replaces the dark photo backdrop: relit, papered over, flattened, sunrise-gradient, photo-as-detail, or dropped entirely.

01Sunlit Photo

Closest to today's build: same full-bleed blurred photo + frosted-glass language, just relit. A bright morning lawn photo, a warm cream tint instead of forest-black, and a soft lens-flare glow in the corner instead of an app-wide vignette.

Flat, opaque colour-blocking instead of glass — no blur, no translucency anywhere. Thick ink outlines and offset "sticker" shadows make every surface feel tappable and forgiving, aimed squarely at someone who's never used a lawn app before.

06Field Manual
