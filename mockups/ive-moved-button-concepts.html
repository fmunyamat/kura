Kura · Settings concept board
"I've moved," six ways.
Six treatments for the lawn-data reset action. All keep the same red "this matters" family — what changes is how much visual weight it carries and how hard it is to trigger by accident, from a quiet text link up to a full warning banner with an explicit confirm step.
01Quiet Link
No card at all — just a small underlined red line under the Data label. Lowest visual weight of the six; reads as "a thing you can do" rather than a button demanding attention.
