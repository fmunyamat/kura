Kura · Today Screen — Collapsing Header

Six ways the header gets out of the way.

Scroll any phone's task list below and watch: the greeting and progress track minimise as the list's top edge scrolls up, and restore fully once you scroll back to the very top. Each concept answers the same question differently — what does "minimised" mean here, and what (if anything) happens to the streak/weather card in between. These are live — scroll inside each phone.

01Shrink & Fade

The literal read: nothing relocates, everything just gets smaller and quieter in place. Greeting shrinks 24px→15px, eyebrow and the streak/weather card fade out, the track's label shrinks to a bare number.
