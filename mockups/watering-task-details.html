Finding your zone times — do this once
1
Put an empty tuna can in zone 1's spray path and run until it's full — write down the minutes
2
Repeat for every zone and note each time separately — heads differ, so times will vary
3
Divide each time by 1 or 2 (your watering days per week) — that's your per-session run time per zone
📌
Your times won't change unless you adjust or replace the sprinkler heads.

Content spec
What goes where, and why
Main card — always visible
Description: "Your lawn needs 1 inch of water a week. Water on 1 or 2 mornings — never more."

Steps:
  1. Run each zone for its set amount of time
  2. Stop if puddles form — move to the next zone
  3. Morning is best — less water evaporates

These steps work for anyone who already knows their zone times. No math, no calibration language.

Details panel — tap to reveal
Link label: "How do I find my zone times?" — a real question, not a generic "More details". Self-selects the people who need it.

Section header: "Finding your zone times — do this once"

Detail steps:
  1. Put an empty tuna can in zone 1's spray path and run until full — write down the minutes
  2. Repeat for every zone and note each time separately — heads differ, so times will vary
  3. Divide each time by 1 or 2 (your watering days per week) — that's your per-session run time per zone
Note: "Your times won't change unless you adjust or replace the sprinkler heads."
Design decision
The link reads "How do I find my zone times?" rather than "More details" or "Learn more". Phrasing it as the user's own question means only people who actually have that question tap it — the rest see clean steps and move on.
