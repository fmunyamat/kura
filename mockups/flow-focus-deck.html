How the deck works

▶ Tap the front card to expand its full details
▶ Click ‹ › to flip through the deck
▶ Click “Done ✓” to complete the front card
1
The front card is the job
Today's first task faces you condensed — photo, title, Done. Tap the card and the description + three plain steps slide open; tap again to tuck them away.
2
Peek without committing
The ‹ › arrows lean the front card aside to reveal what's behind — including tomorrow's locked card. Browsing never completes anything.
3
Stamp & fly
Done slams a DONE stamp onto the card, then it flies off the top of the deck. The card behind springs forward to take its place.
4
Deck cleared
Both of today's cards gone: confetti, streak ticks 6 → 7, and tomorrow's card stays visibly locked at the back — the deck refills overnight.
